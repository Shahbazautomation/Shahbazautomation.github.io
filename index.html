01 — About Me

Investigator first. Builder second.
That order matters.

I've spent 7+ years inside financial-crime investigation with Pakistan's Federal Investigation Agency — working Hundi/Hawala networks, trade-based money laundering, terrorist financing, cybercrime, and human-trafficking cases. Over 300 investigations completed.

Before AML work, I served in the Immigration Wing — screening visa and passport documents for international travellers — and in the Anti-Human Trafficking Circle, pursuing visa fraud and fake travel agencies. Document fraud on one side, financial crime on the other.

Now I build AI automations for exactly those workflows: document verification, transaction monitoring, and investigation reporting. Most automation freelancers learn the tools and guess at the domain. I lived the domain first — I know what a suspicious document looks like because I've held thousands of them.
